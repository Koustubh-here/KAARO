# Agneyum - Full Business Management App for SMBs

### **Vision:**

Empowering small and medium businesses (SMBs) like cafés, boutiques, and local service providers to **digitally manage, grow, and automate** their entire operations — all from a single app.

WHY?

“Because managing a business should be **smart, simple, and scalable** — even from your phone.”

### **Core Features**

### **1. Smart Accounts & Inventory Management**

- Tailored for SMBs (cafés, salons, local stores, etc.)
- 🔹 Track real-time stock & inventory levels
- 🔹 Get **automated alerts** for low stock or expiry
- 🔹 Visual dashboards for daily sales, expenses, and cash flow

---

### **2. Automated Report Generation & Business Analytics**

- 🔹 Downloadable business reports (daily/weekly/monthly)
- 🔹 Visual insights to identify trends & optimize performance
- 🔹 Compare revenue periods and growth charts

---

### **3. Multilingual AI Chatbot Assistant**

- 🔹 Personalized chatbot for **customer service & order queries**
- 🔹 Supports regional languages (great for local businesses)
- 🔹 Acts as a **virtual manager** — suggests action points & follows up on tasks

---

### **4. Smart CRM & Campaign Automation**

- 🔹 Build customer list automatically from sales
- 🔹 Schedule & send **automated marketing messages** (SMS, email, WhatsApp)
- 🔹 Example: "🎉 Diwali Offer - Get 5% OFF!"
- 🔹 Built-in **customer support chatbot**

---

### **5. Social Media Toolkit**

- 🔹 Pre-designed templates to create & post content on Facebook, Instagram, etc.
- 🔹 Auto-sync “Contact Us” and business details with all platforms
- 🔹 Engage with customer reviews & feedback in one place

---

### **6. Personalized Business Card Creator**

- 🔹 Design a digital business card with your brand style
- 🔹 Share it instantly via QR code or link
- 🔹 Embed appointment booking or catalog links
